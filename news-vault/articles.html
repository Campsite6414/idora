January 17, 2008

Discovery Kingdom Extends 2008 Hours

photo

Just as Six Flags announced that they would be cutting their budget across the board, Discovery Kingdom is going in the opposite direction. According to the park's website, hours have been extended all throughout the year and even more operating days. The park will open March 8 at 10:30 AM.

Celebration City's Roaring Falls in 2008

photo

Celebration City in Branson, Missouri has added Roaring Falls to their website. The shoot the chutes style water ride was formerly located at Geauga Lake and Wild Water Kingdom before the park closed in 2007.
